1. Overview & Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how POTKO ("we," "our," or "the Application") handles information when you use the POTKO Android application and our official website (https://potko-app.github.io/potko/). You can browse and stream videos on POTKO without creating an account or submitting your name, phone number, or home address.

2. Information Automatically Processed

To ensure reliable video playback, prevent abuse, and serve standard advertisements, the Application processes limited technical signals:

  • Device & Diagnostics Data: Device model, Android OS version, screen resolution, app version, and anonymized crash logs used strictly to maintain app stability.

3. Third-Party Advertising Partners & SDKs

POTKO is supported by standard in-app video advertisements (such as pre-roll VAST and interstitial/rewarded video placements). Our certified advertising partners may collect device identifiers and ad interaction metrics in accordance with their respective privacy policies:

  • AppLovin Corporation (AppLovin MAX): https://www.applovin.com/privacy/

  • Start.io Inc. (StartApp): https://www.start.io/policy/privacy-policy/

4. User Controls & Opt-Out Rights

You may reset or delete your Android Advertising ID or opt out of personalized advertising at any time via your Android device settings (Settings → Google → Ads). Users in the EEA, UK, and California may also exercise GDPR/CCPA access or deletion rights by contacting us directly.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

POTKO is intended for a general audience aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided personal data to us, contact us immediately for prompt removal.
